About Hearth and Crumb

Hearth and Crumb started in a converted coach house on Millstone Lane with one oven and a sourdough starter that is now over ten years old. We bake in small batches so every loaf leaves the oven at its best.

Our flour is milled by a family mill forty kilometres away. Butter for the laminated pastries is cultured in the dairy next door. We publish our allergen information on every product page.

How pickup works

Place an order online and choose a pickup slot. Orders are reserved as soon as they are placed. Collect at the counter during your slot and pay on collection.
